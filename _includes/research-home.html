A little context

Hello, I'm Shengbang.

I'm an undergraduate at Sun Yat-sen University, studying computer science. In September 2027, I'll join the Embodied Decision Intelligence Lab at Tsinghua University as a PhD student, supervised by Prof. Chao Yu and Prof. Yu Wang.

Previously, I was a research intern at the Human Machine Intelligence Lab, working with Prof. Shanghang Zhang.

My long-term goal is to build general-purpose robot agents that understand, interact with, and create meaningful value in the physical world, paving the way toward Physical AGI.
